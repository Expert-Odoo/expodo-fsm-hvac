Field Service

The Odoo field service module built for HVAC, air conditioning and refrigeration companies. Every unit, every refrigerant charge, every service visit in one place. Install, maintain, repair and invoice without leaving Odoo, and keep an F-Gas record of the gas you handle.

Built for climate engineers

A screen that speaks the trade. Open a service order, log the unit and its refrigerant, pick the job type, work through the steps and invoice. Split, VRF, rooftop, chiller or cold room, each job carries the equipment and the gas it runs on, not a generic service form bent into shape.

Refrigerant and F-Gas, tracked

Every service order records the refrigerant type, the system charge in kilograms, and the gas added or recovered on the visit, with a leak-test flag. The log your technicians need to meet F-Gas obligations is built into the job, not kept on a separate sheet.

See it in action

Every job at a glance

The kanban board lines up each unit by stage, from To Plan through Planned, In Progress and To Invoice. Brand, refrigerant, job type and the technician in charge all show right on the card.

The full equipment and gas record

Equipment type, brand, model, serial and location sit on the order, with the refrigerant, its charge, the gas added and recovered, and the leak-test result right below. Everything about the unit and the job on one screen.

A checklist for every job type

Each job type carries its own steps, ready to use. An F-Gas leak check opens with inspect the joints, run leak detection, record the charge and losses, and update the logbook. The technician ticks as they go and nothing slips through.

From service order to invoice

The order list shows the customer, the brand, the refrigerant, the job and where it stands on billing. One click on Create Invoice turns the labour and parts into an Odoo accounting entry.
