# HVAC & Air Conditioning Field Service

Field service for HVAC, air conditioning and refrigeration companies, built on
Expodo Field Service.

Log each unit, its brand, serial number, refrigerant type and charge, follow the
job from install or breakdown to invoice, keep an F-Gas record of the refrigerant
handled, and let customers track their service online. Ready-made HVAC job types
with their checklists (installation, preventive maintenance, repair, F-Gas leak
check, commissioning).
